Almaty

Explore the beautiful city of Almaty, surrounded by mountains, nature and unforgettable places.

WELCOME TO ALMATY

The City of Mountains

Almaty is one of the most popular destinations in Kazakhstan. The city combines modern life, beautiful nature, culture and amazing mountain views.

It is a great destination for people who enjoy sightseeing, outdoor activities, food and discovering new places.

PLACES TO VISIT

Top Places in Almaty

Medeu

Medeu is a famous mountain skating rink located near Almaty. It is surrounded by beautiful mountains and fresh air. Visitors can enjoy the scenery and spend time in nature.

Learn More

Shymbulak

Shymbulak is a popular mountain resort near Almaty. Visitors can enjoy beautiful mountain views, fresh air and outdoor activities.

Explore

Kok-Tobe

Kok-Tobe is one of the most popular viewpoints in Almaty. Visitors can enjoy a panoramic view of the city and take beautiful photos.

View Place

Big Almaty Lake

Big Almaty Lake is a beautiful mountain lake surrounded by the Trans-Ili Alatau mountains. It is known for its amazing natural scenery.
