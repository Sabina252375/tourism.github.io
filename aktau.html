DISCOVER KAZAKHSTAN

Aktau city

Welcome to Aktau, a beautiful coastal city on the shores of the Caspian Sea in Kazakhstan.

Welcome to Aktau

Aktau is located in the Mangystau Region of western Kazakhstan. It is famous for its beautiful Caspian Sea coastline, sunny weather, modern architecture and unique landscapes.

Aktau is a popular destination for tourists who want to relax by the sea and explore the natural beauty of the Mangystau region. Visitors can enjoy the beaches, watch beautiful sunsets and discover the unusual rocky landscapes around the city.

Nature and Landscape

The main attractions of Aktau are its beautiful Caspian Sea coast, unusual landscapes and desert surroundings.

  • Beautiful Caspian Sea and beaches
  • Desert landscapes and wide open spaces
  • Unique rock formations and canyons
  • Fresh sea air and beautiful sunsets
  • Beautiful views and photo opportunities

Things to do

Visitors can enjoy many activities in Aktau:

  1. Relax on the beaches of the Caspian Sea.
  2. Take a walk along the seaside promenade.
  3. Visit nearby canyons and unusual rock formations.
  4. Take photographs of beautiful sea and desert landscapes.
  5. Watch the sunset over the Caspian Sea.
  6. Explore the city and visit local attractions.

PLACES TO VISIT

Top Places in Aktau\Mangystau region

Bozhyra

is one of the most famous natural attractions near Aktau. It is known for its impressive white cliffs, unusual rock formations and beautiful desert landscapes. Visitors can explore the area, enjoy amazing views and take many memorable photographs. Bozhyra is especially popular with people who love nature and outdoor adventures.

Rocky Trail

is a beautiful place for walking and exploring the natural landscape around Aktau. The area is famous for its rocky paths and unusual geological formations. Visitors can enjoy a peaceful walk, admire the scenery and take photos of the surrounding nature. It is a good place for people who enjoy outdoor activities and discovering new places.

Tetysblu Theme Park

is a popular entertainment park near Aktau where visitors can have an exciting and fun day. The park offers different attractions, water slides and activities for both children and adults. It is a great place to visit with friends or family, especially during the warm summer months.

Laguna Aquapark

is another popular place for relaxation and entertainment in Aktau. Visitors can enjoy swimming pools, water slides and different water activities. It is a perfect place to escape the summer heat, relax with friends or family and spend an enjoyable day near the water.
